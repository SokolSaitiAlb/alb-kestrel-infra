Privacy Policy for Takvimi Kosovë

Last updated: June 10, 2026

​

Sokol Saiti ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Takvimi Kosovë mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

​

1. Data Collection and Usage

Takvimi Kosovë does not collect, store, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information.

  • No Accounts: The App does not require user accounts, emails, or sign-ins to function.
  • No Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics tools or trackers that capture device identifiers.
  • Local Storage: Any application preferences or configurations you modify remain strictly stored on your local device via encrypted or isolated system storage.
​

2. Network and External Links

The App requires internet access solely to synchronize internal utility assets (such as core JSON timing configurations or location-independent updates). No user-specific tokens or tracking metrics are attached to these network operations.

​

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since our App collects no data, it is fully compliant with global child safety standards, including COPPA and GDPR.

​

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
